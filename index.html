👋 Hi there! I’m Krystal.

I’m a PhD candidate in the Human-Centered Computing program at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County, advised by Dr. Foad Hamidi.

Through community-engaged research and participatory design, I work closely with educators, community organizations, families, designers, and people with disabilities to understand how physical environments, emerging technologies such as digital fabrication, facilitation practices, and community partnerships can better support participation. I translate this research into practice by co-creating inclusive programs, tools, and actionable design guidance with the communities they are intended to serve.

📣 I’m actively seeking full-time positions in industry and academia following my PhD graduation in May 2027.
